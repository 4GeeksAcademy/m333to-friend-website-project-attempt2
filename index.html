The journey

A new chapter,
held by old memories.

Vietnam is where his earliest stories began: in the rituals of Lunar New Year, the closeness of extended family, and the small moments of childhood that stay bright for years.

Recently moving to the United States brought a new beginning, and a new understanding of distance. Missing family became part of the story too: a tender reminder that home can travel with us, in the traditions we keep and the memories we share.

✳ Writing for children, and for everyone who has ever missed home.
